FLEETWOOD MAC

EST. 1967 | LONDON

The Architecture of a Legend

A DECADE OF DECADENCE

The transformation of Fleetwood Mac from a blues-rock outfit to a global pop-rock juggernaut stands as one of the most compelling narratives in music history. The 1970s and 80s were marked by a rare alchemy—the intersection of pristine production, raw emotional vulnerability, and complex personal dynamics that birthed timeless masterpieces like Rumours and Tusk.

They didn't just write songs; they crafted an atmosphere. Their sound was the sound of California soft-rock distilled to its purest essence, characterized by Stevie Nicks’ mystical songwriting, Lindsey Buckingham’s obsessive guitar precision, and the unbreakable rhythmic foundation of Fleetwood and McVie.
